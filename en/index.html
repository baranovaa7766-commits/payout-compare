Why the "amount" a prop firm approves isn't what lands in your account

Your prop firm sends your payout in USD (or EUR/GBP), usually via bank transfer, crypto, or a payout partner like RiseWorks. What you actually keep depends on two things once that money is on its way to you: the exchange rate used to convert it into your local currency, and the fees charged along the way. Those two factors can easily swing your final amount by 3-5% — real money on a five-figure payout. This site exists to make that comparison transparent before you pick a method.
