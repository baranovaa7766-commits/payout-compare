How we choose what to show

The calculator on this site ranks payout methods purely by the estimated amount you'd receive, based on publicly available fee and markup information. Whether or not we have an affiliate relationship with a provider has no bearing on that ranking or on the numbers shown.

Where our data comes from

Prop-firm payout terms are collected manually from each firm's own documentation and support resources, and are reviewed periodically — they can still be out of date. Exchange rates come from the free Frankfurter API, which mirrors European Central Bank reference rates. Fee and markup figures for Wise, Revolut, banks, and crypto on-ramps are estimates based on typical published pricing, not live quotes.
